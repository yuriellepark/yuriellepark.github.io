01 / Writing

A Quiet Pencil

100-day poetry project
63 illustrated poems
Forthcoming · Amazon KDP & Apple Books

One page a day, for a hundred days.

Every day Yurielle answered one small prompt with a poem — what I would save first, a rule I wish people would follow, if my thoughts had a map. Each poem was then set as a full illustrated page, so the book reads the way the writing feels: quiet, unhurried, closely observed.

The finished collection is being published as a paperback and e-book under her own name. The whole book can be read here, page by page.

Atlas of My Mind

If my thoughts had a map
There would be crowded roads of thoughts
With dead ends and never ending loops
And in the middle a volcano,
The factory producing endless thoughts and dreams
And somewhere on the side an ocean
Where my dreams float peacefully
Near islands with question marks
With every little thing I don't know.

Infinite Me

A thought I almost said out loud
But that sat at the tip of my tongue
Refusing to be spoken
Was my wish for a clone machine.
If anything like that ever existed
I would make infinite clones of me
And work more wisely
With so much more time
To do even more —
But I wake up from this daydream
Where there's only one of me.

Everything Blue

From the many colors of the rainbow
Red orange yellow green blue and purple
My favorite of all is the color of
The clear sky
And a blueberry pie
The peaceful ocean
And a jar full of blue potion
And also
A bowl of juicy blueberries
And twinkling blue fairies…

02 / Mathematics

Math Team

The Lovett School
Aug 2024 – May 2026
1 hr/week · 26 weeks/yr

Two years on the school math team — meeting weekly to work through competition problems together, then sitting the contests as a team.

It is the same attention the poems ask for, pointed somewhere else: read the problem slowly, notice the one detail everybody skipped, and be willing to sit with not knowing for a while.

Math has rules I trust.

A youth ensemble that rehearses weekly and takes its music out to people who cannot easily come to a concert hall — most often to Mesun Senior Home, where the residents are the whole audience.
